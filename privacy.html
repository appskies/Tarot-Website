2. Information We Collect

We may collect information about you in a variety of ways. The information we may collect via the App includes:

2.1 Personal Data You Provide

  • Profile Information: Name, gender, date of birth (used for horoscope personalization and zodiac calculation), zodiac sign, relationship status, occupation, birth city, goals, personal-growth preferences, and reading preferences you choose to provide. On iOS and Android, profile information is also included in AI requests to personalize readings, as described in Section 10B.

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device Information: Device type, model, operating system and version, unique device identifiers (such as IDFV on iOS, the iOS advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking, or the Android Advertising ID on Android), language settings, and mobile network information.

  • Log Data: IP address, access times, app crashes, and system activity.
  • Purchase Information: Subscription product, store, status, country, and expiration events received from RevenueCat and the Apple App Store or Google Play Store. 2.3 Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do NOT collect precise geolocation data.
  • We do NOT access your contacts, calendar, or other personal files.
  • We do NOT collect your iOS advertising identifier (IDFA) unless you allow tracking in Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt (see Section 14), and we do NOT sell your personal data or share it with data brokers.
  • We do NOT collect payment card information (all payments are processed by Apple or Google).

The following summary is provided to support the Google Play Data Safety section and gives a clear overview of data types collected, their purposes, and whether they are shared.

  • Personal Information (name, date of birth, zodiac sign, gender, relationship status, occupation, birth city, goals, and personal-growth preferences) — Purpose: App functionality, personalization of readings — AI processing: On iOS, profile fields are sent to OpenAI through AIProxy for personalized readings and content moderation. On Android, the same profile fields are sent through our Firebase Cloud Functions to OpenAI (reading text and content moderation) and, for coffee and palm photo readings, to Google Gemini. 10B. AI Processing of Your Profile, Content and Photos

Several Zodya features are powered by third-party artificial-intelligence providers. This section explains, in one place, exactly what is sent, to whom, and what happens to it, so you can make an informed choice before using these features.

  • What triggers AI processing: AI processing is used to provide and personalize AI features — for example, when you add a coffee cup or palm photo and start a reading, ask the AI chat a question, request a spoken (text-to-speech) reading, or generate a soulmate image. On iOS, recent questions and saved summaries may also be processed to maintain personalization context following your use of these features.
  • Coffee & palm photo readings: The photo you capture or select is transmitted over an encrypted connection to our AI vision provider to analyze the image and produce your reading. On Android the provider is Google (Gemini), reached through our secure Firebase Cloud Function. On iOS the provider is OpenAI, reached through our API-gateway provider AIProxy. If the photo cannot be processed, the App may fall back to a text-only reading.
  • AI chat and reading text: The prompts and questions you submit are sent to OpenAI (via our backend on Android, or via AIProxy on iOS) to generate the response. Prompt text is also screened by an automated content-moderation step to prevent misuse.
  • Profile information included in readings (iOS and Android): To personalize AI-generated readings, the App automatically adds the following fields from your saved profile when available: name, gender, date of birth, birth city, relationship status, occupation status, goals, and personal-growth preferences (on Android also your zodiac sign, except in Zodiac Fortune). On iOS these fields are part of the prompt sent through AIProxy to OpenAI for content moderation and reading generation, including text accompanying coffee and palm photos. On Android they are part of the prompt sent through our Firebase Cloud Functions to OpenAI for content moderation and reading generation and, for coffee and palm photo readings, to Google (Gemini) together with the photo. On Android, in the Zodiac Fortune feature, where you choose a sign to read, your own date of birth and zodiac sign are left out of the prompt; on iOS your date of birth is still included in that feature. These fields can be sent even if you do not type them into your question.
  • Conversation and personalization context: On Android, the AI chat includes your recent chat messages in the prompt. On iOS, depending on the feature, requests may also include recent chat questions, a saved summary of recurring concerns from previous conversations, your reading-style preference, and reading-specific details such as selected cards or zodiac signs. OpenAI processes this context through AIProxy to personalize responses and maintain summaries.
  • Soulmate image generation: The text prompt for this feature is sent to Google (Gemini / Generative AI) to produce an illustrative image. On iOS and Android, this prompt includes your saved birth city, the selected gender for the illustration, and a target age calculated on your device using your date of birth, saved gender, and selected illustration gender. On iOS the full text prompt is sent through AIProxy to OpenAI for content moderation and then through AIProxy to Google Gemini for image generation. On Android it is sent through our Firebase Cloud Functions, which check it with OpenAI's moderation service and then send it to Google Gemini; your birth city is also checked on its own.
  • Retention and training: We do not store your photos on our own servers after the reading is generated. Our AI providers do not use API inputs to train their models under the API terms we use with them, and may retain an input only for a limited period (up to about 30 days) for abuse and safety monitoring before deletion, in accordance with each provider's policy.
  • Your control: These features are optional. You do not have to grant camera access, upload a photo, or use the AI chat. Readings are provided for entertainment and self-reflection only and are not professional advice.

14. Tracking, Advertising, and Ad Personalization

14.1 Apple App Tracking Transparency (iOS)

In compliance with Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework:

  • After onboarding, the iOS App asks for your permission through Apple's ATT prompt. The prompt controls whether the App may access your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) and share it with our attribution provider, AppsFlyer.
  • If you allow tracking, AppsFlyer may use the IDFA together with install and app-event data to see which of our ad campaigns brought you to Zodya, by attributing an install, trial, or purchase to the campaign that led to it. This is the only tracking the App performs, and it is not used to show you ads inside the App.
  • If you choose “Ask App Not to Track,” we do not access the IDFA. The App works the same either way. You can change your choice at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • We do NOT use your profile information (such as name, date of birth, or birth city) for tracking or targeted advertising, and we do NOT share your data with data brokers.
  • If we introduce advertising features in the future, we will continue to request your permission through Apple's ATT prompt before any ad-related tracking occurs.

  • The Android Advertising ID is collected for install attribution, marketing campaign measurement (through AppsFlyer), and analytics. We do not use it to show you ads inside the App. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Android settings (usually Settings > Privacy > Ads).
